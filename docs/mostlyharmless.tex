\begin{abstract}
\noindent
These notes derive closed-form complex visibilities for surface brightness maps
expanded in spherical harmonics, in the far-field limit and for a source of unit
angular radius.  Both parity families are treated: $l+m$ odd reduces to a
single spherical Bessel function, and $l+m$ even to a finite sum of ordinary
Bessel functions via a Zernike expansion, the order $m$ being non-negative
throughout.  Conventions are fixed once and for all
in section~\ref{sec:conventions}.  Derivations state their structural steps and
delegate the mechanical algebra to named, machine-checked lemmas; the evidence
rung and the scope of every such claim are stated with it.
\end{abstract}
